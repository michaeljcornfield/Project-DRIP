USE CASE 002

Construction-Integrated Operations Planning

Maintain a feasible operating plan as the railway changes through construction.

THE PROBLEM

The railway you can operate changes over time.

Major construction programs must often be delivered while passenger and freight operations continue.

Work windows, outages, temporary infrastructure configurations, slow orders, and other restrictions continuously change the railway that is actually available to planners.

When infrastructure planning and timetable development occur in disconnected environments, teams risk developing operating plans against infrastructure that is not available during the relevant period.

THE DRIP WORKFLOW

01 Infrastructure State

Maintain the infrastructure representation and the time periods during which configurations and constraints are valid.

→
02 Operating Plan

Develop timetables and routing patterns using only infrastructure that is valid during the relevant planning period.

→
03 Validation

Confirm that the proposed operating plan remains consistent with infrastructure availability and construction constraints.

→
04 Decision

Make service and construction tradeoffs visible to planners, project teams, operators, and the owner.

WHAT CHANGES

From manual coordination to a shared operational basis.

WITHOUT DRIP

Disconnected planning

Infrastructure changes are communicated through meetings, documents, emails, and application-specific updates.

Planners must manually determine which infrastructure assumptions apply to each timetable.

PROPOSED WITH DRIP

Time-valid operational planning

Infrastructure state and operating plans share explicit validity and can be evaluated together.

Changes become visible to the teams and decisions that depend on them.

THE BROADER PATTERN

The application is not the product.

The value comes from maintaining a consistent relationship among authoritative information, specialist applications, organizational workflows, and the decisions they support.

Standardize the interfaces.
Preserve specialization in the applications.
