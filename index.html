PROJECT DRIP · DIGITAL RAIL INTEGRATION PLATFORM

Start with the transport service you need to deliver.

Project DRIP is an evolving approach to connecting rail planning, operations, infrastructure, assets, and performance around the transport service an organization is responsible for delivering.

Data-centric. Service-first. Application-neutral.

THE PROBLEM

Railways don't operate in silos.
Their information often does.

Rail organizations manage complex systems in which infrastructure, operating plans, fleet, performance, capital programs, funding, and organizational responsibilities are deeply interdependent.

FROM IMPLEMENTATION TO PLATFORM

Project DRIP grew out of work we've already been doing.

CRIS began as an effort to help a statewide rail authority connect information that had historically been managed through separate organizations, workstreams, consultants, and applications.

CRIS

Connect the program.

Bring infrastructure, operating plans, fleet, performance, and capital-program information into a common decision environment.

→

RAILPAAS

Make the approach reusable.

Standardize the relationships, integrations, validation logic, and workflows that recur across rail organizations and projects.

→

PROJECT DRIP

Build the orchestration layer.

Connect specialist applications around a common operational representation of the railway while preserving client ownership, open standards, and application choice.

The hypothesis is that these are not separate digital projects. They are different implementations of a common approach to managing the railway as an integrated operational system.

EMERGING USE CASES

Different questions. A common operational core.

USE CASE 001

Fleet & Service Planning

Connect fleet condition and availability with current and future operating plans to understand what service can actually be delivered and what needs to be maintained, overhauled, replaced, or procured.

Fleet State ↔ Operating Plan
Fleet Maintenance Operations Capital

USE CASE 002

Construction-Integrated Operations

Align changing infrastructure states, work windows, outages, and restrictions with tactical timetables so planners only build service against infrastructure that is actually available.

Infrastructure State ↔ Operating Plan
Infrastructure Timetable Validity

USE CASE 003

Performance-Informed Planning

Use observed operations to validate scheduled runtimes, recovery, operating assumptions, and recurring conflicts and feed what is learned back into future operating plans.

Performance → Validate → Operating Plan
Performance Operations Calibration

USE CASE 004

Capital & Strategic Planning

Test future service concepts against fleet, infrastructure, and operational requirements so capital investments remain tied to a clear and validated transport-service need.

Future Service → Requirements → Investment
Strategy Infrastructure Fleet Capital
