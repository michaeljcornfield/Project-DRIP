AN EVOLVING TRANSPORTATION PLATFORM THESIS

Start with the transport service you need to deliver.

TransportPaaS is an approach to connecting transportation planning, operations, infrastructure, assets, and performance around the service an organization is responsible for delivering.

Data-centric. Service-led. Application-neutral.

THE PROBLEM

Transportation doesn't operate in silos.
Its information often does.

Transportation organizations manage complex systems in which infrastructure, operating plans, fleet, performance, capital programs, funding, and organizational responsibilities are deeply interdependent.

The challenge is often not the absence of information. It is connecting that information across organizational and system boundaries so people can make better decisions.

THE OPERATIONAL CORE

Connect the service to the system that delivers it.

In rail, the operating plan provides a common representation of the service and the infrastructure required to deliver it. Performance provides the feedback loop.

Fleet, maintenance, capital programs, finance, and strategy can then connect to this operational core.

THE APPROACH

01

Connect

Bring authoritative information together without requiring every organization to abandon the systems that already work.

02

Validate

Identify inconsistencies between infrastructure, plans, resources, and observed performance.

03

Understand

Make assumptions, dependencies, constraints, and tradeoffs visible across organizational boundaries.

04

Decide

Give transportation staff a common environment for exploring alternatives and making informed decisions.
