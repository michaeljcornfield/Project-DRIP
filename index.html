03 / OPERATIONAL CORE

Plan. Operate. Observe. Calibrate.

The service plan defines what to deliver. Infrastructure defines where and under what constraints. The operating plan reconciles the two into how the railway is intended to run.

↺ Calibration → Operating PlanUse evidence to revise runtimes, recovery, and operating assumptions; revisit service and infrastructure requirements where needed.

Observed operations

Signal data, conductor delay reports, and real-time feeds such as GTFS Realtime can provide evidence of actual operations and performance. Their coverage and quality must be checked.

Planned or future operations

Dynamic simulation can test proposed operations before they happen. Its results remain model estimates, clearly distinguished from observed performance and calibrated against evidence where available.

08 / FIRST PROTOTYPE · PROPOSED EXPERIMENT

Does this infrastructure support this service concept?

Compare an infrastructure export with a service concept for the same scenario and period. Start by making the relationships visible, then check the assumptions that connect them.

EXAMPLE INFRASTRUCTURE INPUT

RailOscope export

Candidate infrastructure objects, connectivity, and availability to map into a shared infrastructure model.

EXAMPLE SERVICE INPUT

Viriato export

Candidate routes, stopping patterns, and timetable information to map into a shared service and operating-plan model.

Standards-aligned exchange → shared models → validation

Test whether exports using railML or other agreed interfaces preserve the identifiers, relationships, units, versions, and validity periods needed for reliable comparison.

  1. Align the inputs. Identify the scenario, source versions, and overlapping validity periods.
  2. Check the paths. Resolve referenced tracks, junctions, and platforms; check connectivity and time-dependent availability.
  3. Show the result. Highlight conflicts and missing information beside the service concept, with traceable evidence for staff review.

ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE · SYNTHETIC DATA

A route uses Track A at 11:00. Track A is closed from 10:00 to 16:00.

Conflict: the service concept requires unavailable infrastructure during that period. Revise the route, timetable, or infrastructure assumptions and check again.

RailOscope and Viriato are example inputs, not platform dependencies or announced integrations. Other tools could participate through compatible exchanges. Export coverage and mappings still need to be tested; missing information must be reported as unknown. These initial consistency checks do not establish full operational feasibility or replace capacity and simulation analysis.
